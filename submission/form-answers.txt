One-line pitch:
BANDIT is the YT trading agent that works while you sleep: it shows where every Pendle YT sits in its own 90-day range, how much room it has to run, and trades your rules on Robinhood Chain only when SERV Reasoning agrees.

Description:
A YT's price moves with its implied APY, yet nobody shows traders whether a YT is cheap or expensive against its own history. BANDIT does. It places every liquid Pendle YT in its 90-day range (P13 means only 13% of days were lower) and turns the range into room to run: what the YT would gain if its rate returned to its 90-day high, and what it would lose back at its low, with days left before decay. Right now, for example, YT-strUSD sits at P13 with +12% to its high and -2% to its low, while tokenized NVDA on Robinhood Chain sits at its 90-day high with nothing left above. For points and airdrop hunters, BANDIT also prices a point: cost per 1,000 points from YT leverage, the points multiplier and decay. Distorted markets (thin, near expiry, extreme APY) are flagged and never ranked.

On top sits an autonomous agent on Robinhood Chain. You set a rule, like entering a YT near the floor of its band and exiting near the top, or switch on Farm mode for points. A watcher checks every 10 minutes. On a trigger it gets a live Pendle quote, and SERV Reasoning must confirm with a written reason before the agent trades from its own wallet. The code enforces hard caps: $25 per trade, $100 per day, 5% maximum price impact, an allowlist, a kill switch, and a simulation before every transaction. Telegram gets the reason, receipts link every trade to the Robinhood Chain explorer, and the Agent Live view animates each run step by step with BANDIT the raccoon narrating.

No wallet is needed to browse, Ask BANDIT, or get Telegram alerts. Reviewers can trade with their own Rabby or MetaMask: BANDIT builds the trade, SERV reviews it, and they sign. Data and reasoning only, not financial advice.

How it uses SERV:
SERV Reasoning is the decision layer, called through the OpenAI SDK at inference-api.openserv.ai/v1 (model gpt-5.4-mini) with strict json_schema output.
1) Risk gate: before the agent acts on a rule, or before a user signs, SERV weighs band position, room to run, decay, liquidity, points data and the live Pendle price impact. It returns confirm or reject with a plain-language reason. Only a confirmed trigger proceeds, and the code still enforces the hard caps on top.
2) Ask BANDIT: a ranked read for the user's size, goal (trade the range, farm points, fixed rate) and risk level.
3) Alerts: a short SERV read attached to every Telegram alert.
There is no fallback model. If SERV fails, the agent does nothing and the UI says so.

Why it has revenue potential:
YT is Pendle's most speculative instrument and the least understood. BANDIT turns it into a readable range with an agent that trades it. Revenue comes from a Pro tier (more rules, faster checks, more markets and chains, custom bands) and an optional small fee per agent trade. Payments can settle in USDG on Robinhood Chain through SUMMON, our x402 facilitator that is already live there, so users pay by signing in their own wallet with no custody. Next come agent accounts for every user: a smart account per user with a capped session key, so anyone can run their own YT agent without handing over keys or funds.
