BANDIT DEMO SCRIPT (about 85 seconds)
Record at 1440 x 900 on https://bandit-bands.vercel.app, with Telegram open on your phone or in a second window.

BEFORE YOU RECORD
1. Agent wallet funded with a little ETH on Robinhood Chain (the Agent tab shows the balance).
2. Telegram: open the bot from "Follow on Telegram" on the Agent tab and press Start.
3. Owner mode unlocked in the browser (Agent tab, Owner mode, your OWNER_KEY).
4. AGENT_LIVE=true set in Vercel and redeployed. Keep the trade small: $10 on a market whose price impact is under 5% (PFE or SHROOM, not NVDA).
5. Warm up once: open Ask BANDIT and ask any question, so SERV's first-call setup is out of the way.

0:00 to 0:10  THE HOOK (Farm tab, top)
Show: the hero with BANDIT asleep in the hammock, and the "cheapest 1K pts" stat.
Say: "People buy Pendle YTs to farm airdrop points, but nobody tells them what a point costs. BANDIT does, and its agent farms on Robinhood Chain while you sleep."

0:10 to 0:24  PRICING POINTS (scroll the Farm board)
Show: the Robinhood Chain cards (tokenized NVDA, PFE, SGOV) with "speculative airdrop, rate unknown", then the priced cards with cost per 1,000 points, points per day, decay and the band gauge.
Say: "Every YT gets one number: cost per 1,000 points, from YT leverage, the points multiplier and decay. Robinhood Chain markets have no published rate, so BANDIT says so instead of inventing one. Distorted markets are flagged and never ranked."

0:24 to 0:36  ARM A RULE (Agent tab)
Show: the rule builder. Band rule, YT-PFE, trigger, action Enter YT, size $10, slippage 3%. Click "Arm this rule". Point at the hard limits card.
Say: "I arm a rule: enter $10 of YT-PFE when its band crosses my trigger. Caps are written into the code: $25 a trade, $100 a day, 5% max price impact."

0:36 to 0:58  THE AGENT WAKES UP (Agent Live)
Show: click Run now. Let the animation play: Markets scan, the rule lights up as triggered, the Pendle quote, SERV Reasoning thinking, then SERV's written reason, Robinhood Chain confirmed, Telegram pinged.
Say: "Normally this runs every 10 minutes. The rule triggers, BANDIT pulls a live Pendle quote, and SERV Reasoning has to confirm with a reason before anything moves. Here is its reason. Confirmed, the trade lands on Robinhood Chain."

0:58 to 1:08  TELEGRAM
Show: the Telegram message: the points math, the size and price impact, SERV's reason, and the tx link.
Say: "Telegram tells me what it did and why, with the points math up front."

1:08 to 1:20  THE RECEIPT (Receipts tab, then the explorer)
Show: the Receipts timeline entry, click the tx link, and show it confirmed on the Robinhood Chain explorer. Back to the points ledger.
Say: "Every trade has an onchain receipt, and the ledger tracks estimated points and decay."

1:20 to 1:28  CLOSE
Show: the Farm tab again, or the Trade with BANDIT sheet with a Rabby connect.
Say: "No wallet needed to browse or get alerts, bring Rabby to trade yourself, and every decision runs on SERV Reasoning. BANDIT: the agent that farms points while you sleep."

IF SERV HOLDS OFF ON CAMERA
That is a feature: show the reason in Agent Live and Telegram ("SERV held off: ..."), then run a rule SERV confirms. The receipts page keeps both.
