BANDIT DEMO SCRIPT (about 100 seconds)
Record at 1440 x 900 on https://bandit.web3wikis.com in Chrome, with Rabby installed.

BEFORE YOU RECORD
1. Warm up SERV once: on Ask BANDIT, tap any suggestion and wait for the answer.
2. My agent: create your free agent with Rabby (it signs a message, no gas). Arm the "first move" rule it suggests (a YT near its floor), so the next run reaches SERV and makes a practice trade.
3. Rabby: keep about $5 of ETH on Base or Arbitrum for the live top-up.
4. Optional real trade by the house agent: Owner mode unlocked, AGENT_LIVE=true set in Vercel and redeployed, a rule armed. Keep it small ($5 to $10). If SERV holds off, that is the risk gate working: show the reason.
5. Optional: Telegram linked from My agent, open on your phone.

0:00 to 0:10  HOOK (Home)
Show: the hero, the raccoon asleep in its hammock, the "near the floor / near the top" numbers.
Say: "A YT's price follows the yield the market expects, but nobody tells you if a YT is cheap or already at its top. BANDIT does, and its agent trades your rules on Robinhood Chain while you sleep."

0:10 to 0:25  MARKETS
Show: Markets, graded A to D, best entries first. Tap the top row: the reasons open in plain words.
Say: "Every YT gets an entry grade from its own 90-day history, the room it has to run, the time left and the pool size. strUSD is cheaper than 87% of its days, with 12% back to its high. NVDA on Robinhood Chain is already at its top."

0:25 to 0:45  ASK BANDIT
Show: Ask BANDIT, tap "Which YTs are near their floor right now?", the four-step progress, then the answer card with the #1 pick. Tap Share card.
Say: "Or just ask. SERV Reasoning reads the live board and answers in plain words, with one-tap actions and a share card for every answer."

0:45 to 1:10  THE AGENT AT WORK (My agent, Agent World)
Show: press Wake my agent. The raccoon walks to the market board, your rules (a check mark), SERV Reasoning (the orb thinks, then turns green), Robinhood Chain (a block drops), Telegram (a paper plane). Then tap Clip it.
Say: "Here is my agent. It checks every 10 minutes on its own. My rule is met, so it asks SERV Reasoning, which has to agree with a written reason before anything moves. I can stream this, or clip it for social."

1:10 to 1:28  TOP UP LIVE (My agent, Top up for real trades)
Show: pick Base and $5, the breakdown (what you send, fees, what arrives, your balance before and after), sign in Rabby, then "Arrived on Robinhood Chain".
Say: "Real trades on Robinhood Chain need only ETH. One signature brings it over from Base in seconds, with every fee shown up front. BANDIT never holds my funds."

1:28 to 1:40  PROOF AND CLOSE (Activity)
Show: Activity, with every check, practice trade and SERV decision. Then Live agent in Stream view.
Say: "Every check and every decision is on the record. BANDIT: the YT agent that works while you sleep, built on SERV Reasoning."

IF SERV HOLDS OFF ON CAMERA
That is the feature: the raccoon looks worried, the reason appears in the bubble and on Activity. Keep it in, then wake it again on a rule SERV confirms.
