Meet BANDIT, a yield-band agent for Pendle PT and YT markets, built on @openservai SERV Reasoning.

It places every live Pendle market on Ethereum and Arbitrum inside its own 90-day implied APY band, so you can see if a rate sits near its floor, mid band, or near its top.
[attach screenshot-1-board.png]

2/3
How it reads a band: live implied APY from Pendle's API, a 90-day min to max band, today's percentile inside it (P12 means near floor), days to maturity, and the 7-day change.

Markets under 14 days old get labelled "band forming" instead of a made-up percentile.
[attach screenshot-2-band-board.png]

3/3
Ask BANDIT: enter a size and a risk level. SERV Reasoning reads the band data and returns a ranked read of the best fits, why each fits, and the main risks.

Data and reasoning only, not financial advice.

Live: https://bandit-bands.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/toteonsol/bandit
