Meet BANDIT, a YT trading agent on Robinhood Chain, built on @openservai SERV Reasoning.

A YT's price moves with its implied APY. BANDIT places every Pendle YT in its own 90-day range and shows the room to run: what it gains back at its high, what it loses at its low.
