How it works

Points are the product. BANDIT puts a price on them.

01

Price every point

YT holders earn points that can turn into airdrops. BANDIT turns multipliers, leverage and decay into one number: what 1,000 points really cost you.

02

Set a rule

Pick a market and a trigger, like band below P20, or switch on Farm mode to chase the cheapest points. Caps are hard-coded: $25 a trade, $100 a day.

03

Sleep on it

The agent checks on a schedule. SERV Reasoning confirms or holds off, the trade lands on Robinhood Chain, and Telegram tells you why.

Robinhood Chain

Where your agent farms

Pendle YTs on Robinhood Chain, including tokenized NVDA, PFE and SGOV. No project here publishes a points rate yet, so BANDIT shows them as speculative airdrop plays with their band and decay, never an invented number.

Priced points

What 1,000 points cost right now

Cost per 1,000 points is the expected YT decay to maturity divided by the points you would earn. Lower is cheaper. Free means the underlying yield should cover the YT cost if rates hold. Distorted markets are shown but never ranked.
