How it works

Every YT has a range. BANDIT knows where it sits.

01

Read the range

BANDIT places each YT's implied APY in its own 90-day range and shows the room to its high and its low, plus days left before decay. For points farmers, it prices 1,000 points too.

02

Set a rule

Enter a YT when it sits near its floor, exit when it reaches its top, or switch on Farm mode for points. Caps are hard-coded: $25 a trade, $100 a day.

03

Sleep on it

The agent checks on a schedule. SERV Reasoning confirms or holds off, the trade lands on Robinhood Chain, and Telegram tells you why.

Where the YTs sit

Room to run is what a YT would gain if its rate returned to its 90-day high today. The move has to happen before maturity, because YT decays as time runs out.

How BANDIT decides

What it weighs for every YT

◐Price vs its history

Where today's price sits against its last 90 days. Near the floor means cheaper than most of those days.

↗Room to run

What it would gain if it got back to its 90-day high, next to what it would lose at its low.

⧗Time left

A YT fades to zero at its end date, so any move has to happen in time.

≈Easy in, easy out

Liquidity: whether your amount can get in and out without moving the price.

✺Interest right now

24-hour volume and the 7-day move show where traders are crowding in.

✦Points and airdrops

A bonus on top, never required. Priced when a project publishes a rate, flagged as a guess when it doesn't.
